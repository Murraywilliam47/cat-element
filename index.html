Cat Element

Five cats, five elemental powers. Cross the world, survive the spikes and the dust bunnies, and take down the Vacuum King.

Meet the cats
Pearl has two forms. Freeze turns ponds into ice to walk on and freezes enemies in place. Cloud lets her ride a cloud over chasms far too wide to jump.
Miley has two forms. Run sprints at top speed, so she can leap the widest rooftop gaps and dash into enemies. Shadow brings out hidden shadow steps, pins enemies down and opens shadow locks. She can always walk through shadow walls.
Quincy has two forms. Scream is a loud fire scream that burns wooden walls and blasts every enemy around her. Chirp calls the birds, who build twig bridges from their birdhouses, and confuses enemies.
Lexus has two forms. Meow is so annoying that enemies pop, even the giant dust bunnies. Leap is his double jump, for walls far too tall for any other cat.
Cookie has two forms. Lion turns him into a big charging lion that can bust through steel walls. Break punches through brick walls and boulders and slams down through concrete floors. He gets tired fast, so watch his stamina bar.
