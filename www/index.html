Cat Element

Five cats, five elemental powers. Cross the world, survive the spikes and the dust bunnies, and take down the Vacuum King.

Meet the cats
Pearl has four forms. Freeze turns water and enemies to ice. Cloud lets her drift over wide gaps. Zap fires lightning at generators to open electric gates. Bird calls her friend to pull high levers and peck enemies.
Miley has four forms. Shadow brings out hidden shadow steps and pins enemies down. Balls throws shadow balls. Run sprints at top speed. Blink teleports her through crystal walls. She can always walk through shadow walls.
Quincy has four forms. Scream lets out a loud fire scream that burns wood and blasts every enemy around her. Climb lets her scale vine walls. Knock topples stone pillars into bridges. Chirp makes a bird sound that leaves enemies confused.
Lexus has four forms. Leap is his double jump. Shine makes him stand still in a beam of light, then blast everything around him. Meow is so annoying that enemies lose it and pop. Storm has him roll in the dust and become a shredding grey storm cloud.
Cookie has three forms. Smash breaks boulders. Break punches through brick walls and slams through concrete floors. Lion turns him into a big charging lion. He is a heavy tank, but he gets tired fast, so watch his stamina bar.
