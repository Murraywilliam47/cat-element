Cat Element Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Cat Element does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.

What the game keeps on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves a small amount of game data on your own device only:

  • which levels you have unlocked, your stars, fish, best times and missions
  • the outfits you chose for the cats
  • your settings, such as volume, difficulty and controls

This data never leaves your device. Deleting the app (or clearing the website's data in your browser) removes it.

Internet use

The game does not send any information over the internet. Once installed, it can be played offline.

Feedback

If you choose to use the in-game Send feedback option, the game prepares a short message (your star rating, any notes you type, the level you were on and the type of device) and opens your device's own share menu. Nothing is sent unless you pick an app and send it yourself, and it goes only to the person you send it to.

"Move my progress" codes

The game can show your progress as a code so you can copy it to another device. The code contains only game progress, and it stays with you unless you share it.

Children

Cat Element is suitable for all ages. Because the game collects no personal information, it does not collect any information from children.

Vibration and sound

The game can use your device's speaker and vibration for game effects. Both can be turned off in Settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer using the contact email listed on Cat Element's App Store or Google Play page.
